Why JalSathi?

Water is essential for daily life, but a large amount of water is wasted through small everyday habits. People often do not think about how much water they use for bathing, washing clothes, cleaning, cooking and gardening. JalSathi helps users understand their approximate household water usage and encourages them to make better water-saving choices.

Small Change, Better Habit

Simple changes in daily life can help reduce water wastage.

❌ Wasteful Habit

Keeping the tap running while brushing teeth or washing utensils.

✓ Better Habit

Turn off the tap whenever water is not immediately required.

❌ Wasteful Habit

Washing clothes in very small loads.

✓ Better Habit

Wait until there is a reasonable load before using the washing machine.

❌ Wasteful Habit

Using clean fresh water when reusable water could be used for suitable purposes.

✓ Better Habit

Reuse suitable household water for activities such as cleaning or gardening where appropriate.

Today's Water Saving Challenge

Try to save at least one bucket of water today.

  • Turn off taps properly after use.
  • Fix leaking taps and pipes.
  • Use a bucket instead of a hose when possible.
  • Reuse suitable water whenever possible.
  • Collect rainwater for plants.
  • Avoid wasting clean drinking water.
